About the Channel

Greg&Ruby is an ongoing fantasy story told through original songs and music videos. Greg is a lute-playing bard who jokes his way through trouble. Ruby is a red dragon with a fierce streak and a past she has to answer for. Their story begins in The Bard Seduced the Dragon, when a hunt for dragon treasure ends with a song and the two of them headed for a wedding.

Their party has its own stories. Bram is a warrior whose friends took an embarrassingly long time to ask his name. Clarence is a cleric who keeps a ledger of everyone’s sins. The Lich Queen has her own reasons to search for the party’s old wizard.

Expect rowdy tavern folk, comic duets, and ballads about love, grief, and second chances. If you’re new here, start with The Bard Seduced the Dragon.
